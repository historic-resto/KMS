Historic Building Stewardship

Some Buildings Are More Than Real Estate.

They are landmarks, workplaces, gathering places, and reminders of the people and industries that built our communities. Owning one is both an investment and a responsibility.

We understand both.

To the Owners Who Care

You became its steward long before anyone called it that.

For years, you have answered the late-night calls, managed the repairs, found the contractors, and solved problems no one else even noticed.

You did not simply own a building. You learned its rhythms, protected its character, and carried the responsibility of keeping it useful.

Eventually, every owner reaches the same point: I need help—but I do not want to lose what makes this place special.

That is why KMS exists.

The KMS Philosophy

Historic buildings require more than maintenance.

01

We Preserve Whenever Possible.

Historic materials should be understood before they are replaced. The most responsible intervention is often the one that retains what already works.

02

We Solve Problems, Not Symptoms.

Water, movement, wear, and previous repairs all leave clues. We look for the cause before recommending the cure.

03

We Think Like Owners.

Every recommendation must balance preservation, practicality, risk, cost, and the long-term life of the building.
